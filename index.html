IMPOSTER

The secret word party game of bluffing, clues and suspicion.

100% offline • Pass & Play • No account required

How to Play

1. Setup
Enter 3–20 player names, choose a category and decide how many imposters you want.

2. Secret Cards
Pass the phone. Every player privately reveals their card. Citizens receive the secret word. Imposters receive their role, category or related word depending on the selected mode.

3. Clues
Every player gets a clue turn. A clue is optional. You can skip individual clues or skip the complete clue phase.

4. Voting
Every player gets a private voting turn. Votes are hidden from the next players. Voting can be skipped completely.

5. Truth Reveal
After voting, the game slowly reveals the actual imposter. Everyone gets to know who the real imposter was.

6. Last Chance
If an imposter was caught, the imposter can attempt to guess the secret word. A correct guess steals the win.
